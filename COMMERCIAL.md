For commercial work involving the new code or new original materials, request separate written permission from the project maintainer. Typical requests include business reports for an employer, paid consulting or training, client deliverables, team use, or embedding the engine in another product. These examples do not override the standard license's organizational permissions or statutory rights.

**Self-service payment is not enabled. There is no published price or automatic commercial grant.** The legal contracting party, fee, applicable version, named users or team, duration, updates, support and effective date must be stated in the agreed written order/license. A GitHub Star, download, or inquiry is not a purchase or license certificate.

## Scope to specify in an order

1. Whether use is for a named individual, an internal team, a client project, or a separately integrated product.
2. Rights to deliver PNG/SVG/PDF/PPTX and interactive HTML, including whether new runtime code can remain embedded for the recipient to view and interact with the delivered report.
3. Whether the recipient can modify that report, generate other reports, redistribute the tool itself, or operate it as a service. Permission for a finished client report does not silently grant resale of the Skill/engine.
4. Update/support period and which formats and editing layers are actually supplied. No all-native editable PPTX, all-device guarantee, or universal quality score is implied.

Your original business data remains yours. We do not claim ownership of it merely because the tool renders a chart. Commercial permission should state the actual output and embedded-code rights; it is not replaced by a marketing statement that 'all outputs are unrestricted'.
